Mac2Seven 1.3.4 makes Keep windows clear of the taskbar smooth, and stops it from shortening windows you move yourself.

## Download
If you have 1.1 or later, Mac2Seven will offer this update on its own (or choose **Check for Updates…** from the menu bar icon's right-click menu). Otherwise, download **Mac2Seven.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Mac2Seven** into your Applications folder. Requires **macOS 14 Sonoma or later**. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## Changes
- **Smooth zooming above the taskbar.** With **Keep windows clear of the taskbar** on and the Dock hidden, Mac2Seven now keeps the Dock underneath the taskbar, sized to match it. macOS then stops zoomed, filled and tiled windows right at the taskbar with its usual smooth animation, instead of Mac2Seven shortening them afterwards. The taskbar gets a solid background in this mode, and your own Dock size, magnification and position come back when you quit Mac2Seven, turn the setting off or choose **Show the Dock Again**.
- **Windows you move stay where you put them.** Dragging or resizing a window so it reaches behind the taskbar no longer shortens it. Only windows that macOS sizes to the bottom of the screen are kept clear.
- On displays without the Dock, windows are shortened only once their zoom animation has finished.
