Mac2Seven 1.3.8 adds the Dock's Finder and Option menu items to the taskbar, and puts Snip in the Start menu.

## Download
If you have 1.1 or later, Mac2Seven will offer this update on its own (or choose **Check for Updates…** from the menu bar icon's right-click menu). Otherwise, download **Mac2Seven.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Mac2Seven** into your Applications folder. Requires **macOS 14 Sonoma or later**. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## New
- **Finder's Dock menu items on the taskbar.** Right-click Finder on the taskbar for **New Smart Folder**, **Find…**, **Connect to Server…** and **Go to Folder…**.
- **Option-right-click, like the Dock.** Hold **Option** while right-clicking a taskbar button for **Relaunch** (Finder), **Force Quit** and **Hide Others**. The normal menu now has **Hide** / **Show** too.
- **Snipping Tool in the Start menu.** It's in the right-hand column, below Activity Monitor, so Snip is easy to reach even with the taskbar turned off. Rename, move or remove it in Settings → Places.
