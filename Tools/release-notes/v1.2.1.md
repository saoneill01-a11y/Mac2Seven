Mac2Seven 1.2.1 polishes the new taskbar and adds a calendar to its clock.

## Download
If you have 1.1 or 1.2, Mac2Seven will offer this update on its own (or choose **Check for Updates…** from the menu bar icon's right-click menu). Otherwise, download **Mac2Seven.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Mac2Seven** into your Applications folder. Requires **macOS 14 Sonoma or later**. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## New in 1.2.1
- **Clock calendar.** Click the time on the taskbar for a calendar with a live clock (hours, minutes, seconds and AM/PM) and today's date. Use the arrows to change month, or click the month name to jump back to today. Choose **Mac2Seven glass**, **Windows 11** or **Compact** in **Settings → Taskbar → Clock calendar**.
- **Start menu from the taskbar.** Opened from the taskbar's Start button, the menu puts **All Programs** and the search box at the bottom, like Windows. Opened from the menu bar or with ⌥Space it's unchanged.
- **Apps with no open windows.** Mac apps keep running after you close their last window. With **Combine (with app names)**, they now shrink to an icon instead of keeping a full-width button. Turn on **Hide apps with no open windows** in Settings → Taskbar to remove them from the taskbar entirely (pinned apps stay).
- **Hide the Dock** now keeps the Dock from popping up when the pointer reaches the bottom of the screen. **Show the Dock Again** puts back your own Dock setting.

## Fixes
- A window preview no longer covers a button's right-click menu.
