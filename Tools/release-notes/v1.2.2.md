Mac2Seven 1.2.2 fixes the taskbar on Macs with more than one display.

## Download
If you have 1.1 or later, Mac2Seven will offer this update on its own (or choose **Check for Updates…** from the menu bar icon's right-click menu). Otherwise, download **Mac2Seven.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Mac2Seven** into your Applications folder. Requires **macOS 14 Sonoma or later**. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## Fixes
- **Taskbar on several displays.** With two or more monitors (including DisplayLink displays), hovering over the taskbar did nothing: buttons didn't highlight and window previews didn't appear. Each display's taskbar now tracks the pointer on its own, so hover highlights and previews work on every display.
