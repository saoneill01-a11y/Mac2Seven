Mac2Seven 1.3.5 shows notification badges on taskbar icons and makes the Dock-under-the-taskbar mode optional.

## Download
If you have 1.1 or later, Mac2Seven will offer this update on its own (or choose **Check for Updates…** from the menu bar icon's right-click menu). Otherwise, download **Mac2Seven.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Mac2Seven** into your Applications folder. Requires **macOS 14 Sonoma or later**. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## New
- **Notification badges.** Taskbar icons now show the same red badge as the Dock, such as the number of unread messages in Messages.

## Changes
- **Smooth zoom is now a choice.** Keeping the hidden Dock under the taskbar (new in 1.3.4) gives the smoothest zoom, but the Dock's app names can pop up when the pointer is over the taskbar. It's now off by default. Turn it on with **Smooth zoom: keep the hidden Dock under the taskbar** in Settings → Taskbar.
- With the Dock hidden, zoomed windows are now trimmed to the taskbar right after their zoom animation, instead of up to a second later.
