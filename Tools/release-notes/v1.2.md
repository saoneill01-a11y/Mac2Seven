Mac2Seven 1.2 adds an optional Windows-style taskbar along the bottom of the screen, with the Start menu on its Start button.

## Download
If you have 1.1, Mac2Seven will offer this update on its own (or choose **Check for Updates…** from the menu bar icon's right-click menu). Otherwise, download **Mac2Seven.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Mac2Seven** into your Applications folder. Requires **macOS 14 Sonoma or later**. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## New in 1.2: the taskbar
Turn it on in **Settings → Taskbar**.

- **Start button.** The Mac2Seven icon sits at the start of the bar and opens the Start menu just above it.
- **Your apps.** Pinned apps and everything that's running, with a dot under running apps and a blue line under the one in front. Click to open an app, bring a window forward, or minimize it.
- **Window previews.** Hover over a button to see its windows. Click one to switch to it, or close it with its ×.
- **Right-click menu.** Open a new window, pin or unpin, minimize or restore, close windows, or quit the app.
- **Three button styles.** Icons only; one button per app with its name and a window count; or one labeled button per window.
- **Room for everything.** Use 1 to 3 rows, centered or left-aligned. When the bar fills up, labels get narrower, apps with several windows combine, and the rest go into a ⌃ menu at the end.
- **Show desktop.** Click the thin strip at the far right to hide every app, and click again to bring them back. The clock sits beside it.
- **Stays out of the way.** Windows that reach behind the taskbar are made shorter, and **Hide the Dock** keeps the Dock from covering it.

## Permissions
The taskbar asks for two permissions, and **Settings → Taskbar** shows whether each is on:
- **Accessibility** lets it list, minimize, restore and close windows. Until it's allowed, a yellow ⚠︎ appears next to the clock.
- **Screen Recording** shows live window previews. Without it, previews show the app's icon.

If you turned on Accessibility for an earlier test copy and the ⚠︎ stays, remove Mac2Seven from the Accessibility list in System Settings and add it again.
